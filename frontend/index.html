Predictive Ghost Warrants

Each warrant fuses every independent signal for one axle. Escalation to URGENT REVIEW requires at least two independent signals to agree — a single signal, even a severe one, is held at REVIEW.

AxiomDynamics never silently translates "URGENT REVIEW" into a stop-train instruction. Existing railroad operating rules, detector logic, and authorized personnel remain sole authority over train movement.

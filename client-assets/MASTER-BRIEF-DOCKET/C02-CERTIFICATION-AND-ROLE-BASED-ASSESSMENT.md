# C02 — Certification & Role-Based Assessment

**Docket position:** C02. The training and competency framework for the Railroad's personnel. D02 §5 requires certified reviewers; this document defines what "certified" means.

> **Drafting status:** Commercial grade. Must be reviewed and adopted by the Railroad's training and HR departments, and by AxiomDynamics' learning team before rollout. Role definitions and competency matrices are provided as a base framework; the Railroad may extend or refine them per its internal standards.

---

## 1. Overview

Certified personnel are authorized to record dispositions in the AxiomDynamics platform. **Certification is not a safety appliance approval, a regulatory compliance statement, or a guarantee of individual performance.** It evidences completion of AxiomDynamics' training curriculum and demonstrated competency against that curriculum.

**Scope of this document:**
- Role definitions (Operator, Reviewer, Administrator, Safety Officer)
- Competency matrix for each role
- Assessment methods and scoring
- Certification validity, renewal, and revocation
- Certificate issuance and registry
- Railroad responsibility for supervision and ongoing compliance

---

## 2. Roles and responsibilities

### 2.1 Operator

**Definition:** an individual who monitors the platform, reviews advisories generated by the system, and supplies information to a Certified Reviewer.

**Does not record dispositions.** Acts as the first point of contact for the platform's output.

**Responsibilities:**
- Monitor the platform dashboard and alert queue daily.
- Identify advisories that warrant escalation to a Reviewer.
- Gather contextual information (train composition, consist history, recent maintenance) to support the Reviewer's decision.
- Document the status of each advisory in notes (not dispositions).
- Alert the Reviewer and the Safety Officer of any recurrent patterns or suspected data anomalies.

**Access level:**
- Read-only access to the platform: advisories, historical reports, trend analysis.
- No write access to dispositions or system configuration.
- No access to underlying code or models.

**Competency areas:**
- Basic platform interface navigation.
- Understanding of advisory ranking (severity and confidence tiers).
- Railroad's own detector infrastructure (spacing, types, maintenance schedule).
- Incident reporting and escalation procedures.
- Data hygiene: recognizing malformed or suspicious detector output.

**Prerequisites:** current employee or contractor of the Railroad; familiarity with rail operations or locomotive maintenance; no formal prerequisites.

### 2.2 Reviewer (Certified)

**Definition:** an individual authorized to review advisories and record dispositions. Only Certified Reviewers may record dispositions in the platform.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review each advisory presented by the platform.
- Assess the advisory in the context of the detector data, recent maintenance history, consist composition, and operating conditions.
- Record a disposition (accept, defer, or reject) with brief justification.
- Escalate anomalies or recurrent patterns to the Safety Officer.
- Maintain accurate, timely disposition records.

**Access level:**
- Read and write access to advisories and dispositions.
- Read access to historical data, reports, and detector logs.
- No access to system configuration, code, or models (except as provided in training).

**Competency areas:**
- All Operator competencies (§2.1).
- Detailed understanding of bearing temperature physics and failure modes.
- Interpretation of wayside detector output and limitations.
- Integration of detector data with consist and maintenance history.
- Understanding of the platform as a decision-support tool, not a directive or final authority.
- Ability to recognize and document false positives and false negatives.
- Communication of disposition rationale (written justification).

**Prerequisites:**
- Current employee or contractor of the Railroad.
- At least **twelve (12) months** of experience in a railroad inspection, engineering, or maintenance role, or equivalent demonstrated knowledge.
- Successful completion of the Reviewer certification assessment (§3.2).
- **Must not have a disciplinary record for falsifying inspection records or evidence in the past 24 months.**

**D02 requirement:** every advisory reviewed by a Certified Reviewer is recorded with a SHA-256 fingerprint of the advisory, the timestamp, and the Reviewer's signature (digital where issued, otherwise handwritten), together with the timestamp of the disposition.

> **What this record does and does not prove today.** It proves the record's *content* has not changed since it was written, and — since 2026-11-09 — that the disposition history is append-only: a correction is a new record referencing the one it corrects, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` are refused by the database, and every record is hash-chained to its predecessor (AD-3, B01 §4.1/§4.6). Since 2026-11-11 it also proves *which credential* wrote it: a disposition requires a live session bound to a registered certification, and the name on the row comes from that session rather than from the request (AD-2, B01 §7 item 1). What it does **not** prove is that a *person* was at the keyboard — the claim is about a credential holder, and binding one to a person is key custody, a procedure rather than code. Nor is the ledger signed or replicated off-box, so a party with write access to the database file could rewrite it and recompute the chain. Represent the append-only property as built; do not represent the record as a signed, provable-identity audit trail until AD-2 and the signed export close.

### 2.3 Administrator

**Definition:** an individual responsible for system configuration, user account management, platform updates, and operational continuity.

**Does not typically review advisories** (though may do so if a Certified Reviewer in their own right).

**Responsibilities:**
- Install and maintain the platform software (per C01).
- Manage user accounts, access levels, and password/credential policies.
- Apply security updates and patches promptly (per D06).
- Monitor system health (uptime, database size, listener status).
- Generate and archive reports.
- Perform backups and test disaster recovery (per §5.2 of this document).
- Audit access logs and identify anomalies.
- Escalate system failures or security incidents immediately.

**Access level:**
- Full administrative access to the platform, including configuration, user management, and system logs.
- Access to the database and underlying files (for backup and recovery).
- No access to modify source code or core logic (that is AxiomDynamics' responsibility).

**Competency areas:**
- All Operator competencies (§2.1).
- Linux system administration (package management, filesystem, user permissions, systemd or equivalent).
- Database concepts (SQLite schema, query basics, backup/restore).
- Network and firewall configuration.
- Security best practices (credential management, audit logging, incident response).
- Platform architecture and deployment options (standard, air-gapped, containerized).

**Prerequisites:**
- Current employee of the Railroad (not contractors, as this role involves system administration).
- At least **two (2) years** of Linux system administration experience, or demonstrated proficiency.
- Successful completion of the Administrator certification assessment (§3.3).

### 2.4 Safety Officer

**Definition:** an individual responsible for safety oversight, regulatory compliance, and escalation of safety-significant anomalies.

**May or may not be a Certified Reviewer**, depending on the Railroad's organizational structure. Often the Chief Engineer or Roadmaster.

**Responsibilities:**
- Review disposition records and trend analysis monthly.
- Escalate recurrent patterns or anomalies to Operations.
- Coordinate with Reviewers and Operators on data integrity concerns.
- Maintain compliance with all regulatory obligations (FRA, Transport Canada, ARTF, etc.).
- Ensure the platform is not used as a substitute for required inspection or regulation compliance.
- Serve as the primary contact for questions about D12 (regulatory status).
- Maintain records of certification compliance and renewal schedules.

**Access level:**
- Read-only access to advisories, dispositions, and reports.
- Access to audit logs and certification registry.
- Escalation hotline for safety or compliance concerns.

**Competency areas:**
- Regulatory framework (D12 of this docket, FRA rules, Transport Canada rules as applicable).
- Understanding of the platform's constraints and limitations.
- Railway safety and operations fundamentals.
- Incident investigation and root-cause analysis.

**Prerequisites:**
- Current employee of the Railroad in a safety, engineering, or operations leadership role.
- Demonstrated knowledge of applicable regulatory obligations.
- No formal prerequisites for other certifications (may be a Reviewer in parallel if desired).

## 3. Assessment and certification

### 3.1 Assessment structure

Each role (Operator, Reviewer, Administrator, Safety Officer) is assessed on a pass/fail basis against a competency matrix. **There are no partial certifications.** A candidate either meets the threshold or does not.

**Assessment methods:**

| Method | Role | Format | Duration | Passing score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Online knowledge test | Operator, Reviewer, Administrator | Multiple choice, scenario | 60–90 min | 80% |
| Hands-on scenario | Reviewer | Review 5–10 sample advisories and record dispositions | 45–60 min | 80% (reasoning must be sound; not all decisions must match "expected" answer) |
| Hands-on lab | Administrator | Deploy the platform from scratch, configure users, apply an update, test recovery | 120 min | Pass/fail (must complete all tasks without critical errors) |
| Structured interview | Safety Officer | Discussion of regulatory obligations, policy, and escalation scenarios | 30–45 min | Pass/fail (assessor evaluates knowledge and judgment) |

### 3.2 Reviewer assessment — detailed

The Reviewer assessment is the most rigorous, as Reviewers record the dispositions that form the basis of the platform's credibility.

**Knowledge test (30 min, 40 points):**
- Bearing failure modes and indicators (physics, not memorization)
- Interpretation of detector outputs and their limitations
- Platform ranking logic and confidence tiers
- D12 requirements and regulatory limits
- Scenario-based questions: *"You see an advisory classified as 'High Risk'. The detector maintenance record shows it was calibrated 90 days ago. The consist history shows a bearing replacement last month. What do you record as your disposition and why?"*

**Hands-on scenario (45–60 min, 60 points):**
- Candidates review a prepared dataset: 5–10 actual or synthetic advisories with supporting detector logs, maintenance records, and consist information.
- For each advisory, the candidate records a disposition (accept, defer, or reject) and writes a brief justification.
- Scoring focuses on the **reasoning**, not on whether the decision matches a predetermined "correct" answer. A well-reasoned decision based on the data is passing, even if a different Reviewer might have decided differently.
- Borderline judgements are evaluated for soundness of logic, not for matching an oracle.

**Passing threshold:** 80% on both knowledge test and hands-on combined (i.e., at least 80 of 100 total points).

### 3.3 Administrator assessment — detailed

**Knowledge test (30 min, 30 points):**
- Linux filesystem and permissions
- Package management (apt, yum, or equivalent)
- Systemd or other service management
- Basic SQL concepts (SELECT, WHERE, basic schema)
- Backup and disaster recovery concepts
- Firewall and network isolation
- Security best practices (credential management, audit logs)

**Hands-on lab (120 min, 70 points):**
- Candidate deploys the platform on a test server using either standard or air-gapped method.
- Candidate creates two user accounts (one Reviewer, one Operator) and sets permissions.
- Candidate applies a simulated security patch and restarts the service without downtime (if possible).
- Candidate backs up the database and verifies restore.
- Candidate reviews system logs and identifies a simulated incident (e.g., a failed login attempt, a connection error).

**Passing threshold:** must complete the lab without critical errors (no unrecovered data loss, no service misconfiguration, no security mistake such as leaving credentials in plaintext).

### 3.4 Assessment scheduling and retakes

- Assessments are offered on a scheduled basis (e.g., monthly) and can be booked in advance.
- A candidate who does not achieve the passing threshold may retake the assessment, but not sooner than 14 days after the first attempt.
- A candidate who fails three times is considered to lack sufficient aptitude for the role and is advised to pursue a different role or training.
- Results are kept confidential and shared only with the candidate and the Railroad's training coordinator.

## 4. Certification validity and renewal

### 4.1 Certificate lifetime

Each certification is valid for **three (3) years** from the date of issuance.

At the end of year 2, the Certified individual receives a renewal notice and is invited to complete a renewal assessment. The renewal assessment is a condensed knowledge check (30 min, 20 questions) focused on any changes to the platform, regulations, or role responsibilities. If the individual passes, the certificate is extended for another 3 years. If not, the certificate expires on the scheduled date.

### 4.2 Immediate revocation (emergency)

A certification may be revoked immediately without notice if the individual:
- Knowingly records a false disposition (verified by audit).
- Violates the confidentiality or security policy (e.g., shares platform credentials, exports data without authorization).
- Is disciplined for a safety violation by the Railroad.
- Is placed on administrative or legal leave that prohibits them from performing their duties.

On revocation, the individual loses access to the platform and all associated data within 24 hours. The Railroad notifies AxiomDynamics of the revocation so that the certification registry is updated.

### 4.3 Voluntary revocation

An individual may request voluntary revocation at any time (e.g., on resignation, transfer, or retirement). Revocation is effective immediately, and access is terminated.

## 5. Certification registry and audit

### 5.1 Registry management

AxiomDynamics maintains the master registry of all Certified personnel. The registry includes:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Name | Full legal name |
| Railroad | Employing railroad |
| Role | Operator / Reviewer / Administrator / Safety Officer (or multiple) |
| Certification date | Date the assessment was passed |
| Validity expires | Three years from certification date |
| Status | Active / Expired / Revoked / Suspended |
| Assessment score | Knowledge test and (if applicable) hands-on score |
| Assessor | Name and date of the person who conducted the assessment |

The registry is stored in a secure, encrypted database with audit logging. Access is restricted to AxiomDynamics' learning team and to the Railroad's designated training coordinator.

### 5.2 Access and verification

- The Railroad may query the registry at any time to confirm an individual's certification status.
- A Certified individual receives a digital certificate (PDF with QR code and verification details) at the time of issuance.
- Third parties (e.g., FRA inspectors, insurance carriers) may request verification of a specific individual's certification. Verification is provided only with the individual's written consent.

### 5.3 Audit and compliance

Quarterly, AxiomDynamics provides the Railroad with a compliance summary:
- Number of Operators, Reviewers, Administrators, and Safety Officers currently certified.
- Number of certifications expiring in the next 90 days.
- Number of certifications revoked in the past quarter.
- Any anomalies or trends.

The Railroad is responsible for ensuring that its personnel maintain current certifications and for scheduling renewals.

## 6. Training materials and delivery

### 6.1 Content

AxiomDynamics provides the following training materials:

| Material | Role | Format | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator handbook | Operator | Written guide + video | PDF + online portal |
| Platform user guide | Operator, Reviewer | Step-by-step UI walkthrough | Interactive web-based + PDF |
| Physics fundamentals | Reviewer | Technical deep-dive: bearing mechanics, failure modes, detector physics | Recorded video (2–3 hours) |
| Case study collection | Reviewer, Safety Officer | 10–15 real (anonymized) or synthetic scenarios with expert commentary | PDF with video walkthrough |
| Administrator deployment guide | Administrator | Hands-on lab manual; covers all three deployment methods | C01 (this docket) |
| Regulatory briefing | Safety Officer, Reviewer | Overview of D12, FRA rules, Transport Canada rules, and the platform's status | Recorded video (1–2 hours) + PDF |

### 6.2 Delivery

Training is delivered via:
- **Synchronous (live instructor-led):** scheduled sessions conducted online or in-person. Best for Administrator certification and Reviewer scenarios.
- **Asynchronous (on-demand):** recorded videos, interactive modules, and reading materials. Allows flexibility for distributed teams.
- **Blended:** combination of self-paced learning followed by a live session for Q&A and assessment.

The Railroad selects the delivery method and provides the venue/infrastructure. AxiomDynamics provides the content and conducts assessments.

### 6.3 Timeline

For a single-site pilot (D02):
- Week 1: training materials released to Reviewers and Operators.
- Week 2–3: self-paced learning and optional live Q&A.
- Week 4: knowledge test and assessment.
- Week 4–5: hands-on labs (if applicable).
- Week 5: certification issued, access granted, platform goes live.

**Total time per Reviewer:** 8–12 hours (study + assessment). **Total time per Operator:** 4–6 hours. **Total time per Administrator:** 20–30 hours (including lab time).

## 7. Representational discipline

**The platform is a decision-support tool. Certification evidences that an individual has completed training and assessment, not that they are infallible.**

The following statements are **prohibited:**

- *"AxiomDynamics certified personnel can guarantee detection of defects"* — Certification does not imply guarantee. Platform output may be missed or misinterpreted.
- *"Certification is a FRA/Transport Canada credential"* — No. It is an AxiomDynamics credential only.
- *"Certified Reviewers are responsible for all maintenance decisions"* — Reviewers record dispositions; operating decisions remain with the Railroad.

**Accurate statements:**

- *"All personnel using the AxiomDynamics platform have completed our training and passed a competency assessment."*
- *"Certified Reviewers record dispositions in accordance with their training."*
- *"The platform supports the Railroad's existing inspection and maintenance processes."*

## 8. Railroad responsibility

The Railroad is solely responsible for:
- Selecting personnel for training and determining fitness for the assigned role.
- Scheduling training and ensuring attendance.
- Maintaining a current roster of Certified personnel and notifying AxiomDynamics of changes (new certifications, revocations, expirations).
- Supervising Certified personnel and ensuring they perform their duties in accordance with this training framework and the Railroad's policies.
- Investigating any instance in which a Certified individual is suspected of recording false dispositions or violating policy, and taking corrective action.
- Arranging renewals and retraining before certifications expire.
- Disciplining or removing access for any individual who violates confidentiality, security, or regulatory obligations.

**AxiomDynamics does not:**
- Supervise the Railroad's personnel.
- Investigate internal discipline matters.
- Terminate or restrict access based on the Railroad's personnel decisions (though the Railroad may request revocation, which AxiomDynamics executes).

## 9. Special considerations: Canadian carriers

In Canada, Transport Canada's *Rules Respecting Key Trains and Key Routes* require documented inspections at regular intervals. **Certification of AxiomDynamics Reviewers strengthens the credibility of the disposition record as supporting evidence that the Railroad has given documented attention to bearing condition.**

However, **certification does not substitute for required WDBD inspection, and disposition records are not regulatory inspection records.** See D12 §3 for the full disclaimer.

The value of certification in the Canadian context is:
1. **Evidential:** the disposition record shows the Railroad's considered attention to the information its detectors produce.
2. **Process integrity:** Certified Reviewers are trained in the platform's logic and limitations, reducing the risk of misinterpretation.
3. **Audit trail:** signed, timestamped dispositions create a clear record for post-incident investigation.

## 10. Appendix: Sample competency matrix

### Operator competency checklist

| Competency | Not competent | Developing | Competent | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platform interface navigation | Cannot locate advisories or reports | Can navigate with reference material | Navigates independently and efficiently | Live demo: find a specific advisory, pull historical data |
| Understanding advisory ranking | Cannot explain confidence or severity tiers | Can identify high/medium/low ranking | Can interpret ranking in context and explain implications | Written question: what does "High severity, medium confidence" mean operationally? |
| Detector knowledge | Cannot name detector types or spacing | Can recall detector spacing at pilot site | Understands spacing, maintenance intervals, failure modes | Discussion: if HBD spacing is 60 miles on this subdivision, where are the gaps? |
| Escalation procedure | Does not know how to escalate | Can escalate with prompting | Independently identifies and escalates anomalies | Scenario: a series of advisories in the same spot; what do you do? |
| Data hygiene | Cannot identify anomalies | Can spot obvious errors with guidance | Recognizes malformed or suspicious data and escalates | Scenario: detector reports bearing temp of 500°C; what is your assessment? |

### Reviewer competency checklist (sample questions)

| Competency | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Bearing physics | Knowledge test: name three failure modes of roller bearings and their precursors. |
| Detector interpretation | Knowledge test: detector shows gradual temp rise over 3 weeks, then drops suddenly. Interpret and explain. |
| Platform logic | Hands-on: review advisory rated "High severity, low confidence." Given the supporting data (detector history, maintenance record, consist info), record your disposition and justify. |
| Regulatory awareness | Knowledge test: Under Transport Canada Key Trains rules, what is the maximum distance a Key Train can travel without WDBD inspection? Is AxiomDynamics a substitute? Explain why or why not. |
| Documentation | Hands-on: for three test advisories, write concise disposition justifications that would satisfy an auditor. |
| Risk judgement | Scenario question: two advisories, same bearing; second is newer and more confident. Your disposition on the first was "defer." Do you change it? Why or why not? |

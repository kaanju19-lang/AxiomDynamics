# F11 — Site Visit Report

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F11 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years**

**Purpose:** to record what we observed on site, what we did, **and what we declined to do**.

**Completed within 5 Business Days of return.** Required before the travel expense claim (F07) may be submitted.

> **This form is the record that keeps the liability boundary intact.**
>
> A site visit is the highest-risk activity we undertake, because presence creates the appearance of involvement. A vendor standing in a data center during an installation looks like an installer. A vendor in a meeting about a hot bearing looks like an adviser. This form records, contemporaneously, what our role actually was — and the declinations are as important as the observations.

> "The Platform shows the assessment and how it was computed. Whether to act on it is your operating decision under your rules. I can tell you what the Platform computed; I cannot tell you whether to move the train."

**Number of boundary events this visit:** ______

> **A form with zero boundary events is normal.** A form with several is not a problem — it is a record that the boundary was tested and held. The problem is a visit with no record at all, because then the only account of what was said is someone's memory, and memory is always reconstructed in the direction that benefits the person remembering.

## Part 6 — Exceptions and matters for escalation

Anything observed that affects safety, regulatory compliance, or the contractual position — record it, even where it is uncomfortable.

| # | Matter | Why it matters | Raised with | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | |

> **If we observe something safety-relevant, we say so — in writing, to a named individual.** Not because we have a duty to inspect, and not because we are accepting one, but because staying silent about an observed hazard would be indefensible and would also be wrong. **Record that we raised it and that we are not the inspecting party.**

**Statement to include in any safety-relevant escalation:**

> "This observation is recorded as part of our advisory role. We are not an inspecting party and this does not discharge any inspection, testing, or regulatory obligation of the Customer (D12 §3). We recommend it be handled under the Customer's own procedures by its own qualified personnel."

# D11 — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Docket position:** D11. Executed **before** D02 and before any technical or commercial discussion. Provided as a standard form; changes require agreement of both parties.

> **Drafting status:** Form of agreement. Requires counsel review before first execution.

---

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made between **AxiomDynamics** ("AxiomDynamics") and **__________________** ("Railroad"), each a "Party" and together "the Parties", effective on the date of the last signature below.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate a potential commercial relationship concerning predictive maintenance software for railway operations ("Purpose"). Each Party may disclose confidential information to the other for that Purpose only.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient"), in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable recipient would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

**Includes, without limitation:**

*Disclosed by AxiomDynamics:* source code, object code, physics models, equations, constants, thresholds, algorithms, ranking logic, architecture, security design, documentation, pricing, rate cards, the contents of the Master Brief Docket, cost structures, commercial strategy, and the existence and content of any pilot negotiation.

*Disclosed by Railroad:* network topology, detector locations and spacing, operating data, incident and derailment records, internal procedures, personnel information, and any safety or security assessment.

**Excludes** information that: (a) is or becomes public through no breach; (b) was lawfully known to Recipient before disclosure without restriction; (c) is received from a third party without restriction and without breach; (d) is independently developed without use of or reference to the Confidential Information; or (e) is approved for release in writing.

## 3. Obligations

Recipient shall: use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose; protect it with at least the care it uses for its own comparable information and no less than reasonable care; disclose it only to those of its personnel and professional advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by equivalent obligations; and not copy it except as necessary for the Purpose.

**Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software or technical material disclosed.**

## 4. Compelled disclosure

If Recipient is required by law, regulation, court order or regulatory authority to disclose, it shall give Discloser prompt written notice where lawfully permitted, disclose only the portion required, and reasonably cooperate in any effort to obtain protective treatment.

**Note for both Parties:** railway safety regulators may have statutory access rights that override notice obligations. Nothing in this clause restrains a Party from complying with a lawful regulatory demand.

## 5. No licence, no obligation

No licence, ownership or other right in any intellectual property is granted by disclosure. **All intellectual property disclosed by AxiomDynamics remains the sole property of AxiomDynamics** — see D13.

Nothing in this Agreement obliges either Party to proceed with any transaction, to disclose any particular information, or to refrain from developing or acquiring competing products, **provided** that a Party does not use the other's Confidential Information to do so.

## 6. Term and survival

This Agreement runs for three (3) years from the effective date. The obligations of confidentiality survive for **five (5) years** from the date of each disclosure, and **indefinitely for trade secrets** for so long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 7. Return or destruction

On written request or on termination of discussions, Recipient shall return or destroy Confidential Information and certify destruction in writing, except that one copy may be retained where required by law or by a Party's document retention policy, subject to continuing confidentiality.

## 8. No warranty

Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party makes any warranty as to its accuracy or completeness, and neither Party shall be liable for any reliance on it.

## 9. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that damages may be an inadequate remedy for breach of this Agreement, and that the Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without the necessity of proving actual loss or posting security.

## 10. Residual knowledge

Nothing in this Agreement restricts either Party from using the unaided memory of its personnel, provided such use does not involve reproduction of documents or disclosure of specific technical detail. **This clause does not permit use of disclosed source code, models, constants or pricing structures.**

## 11. Governing law and disputes

Governed by the laws of __________________, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Disputes resolved by __________________.

*Drafting note: for a US counterparty, a neutral commercial venue in the State of __________________ is usual. Avoid the counterparty's home state where the balance of negotiating power permits.*

## 12. General

Entire agreement on its subject; supersedes prior understandings on confidentiality. Amendment only in writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder survives. Neither Party may assign without written consent, save to a successor by merger, reorganisation or sale of substantially all assets, subject to assumption of obligations.

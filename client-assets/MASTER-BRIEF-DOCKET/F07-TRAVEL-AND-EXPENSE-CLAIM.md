# F07 — Travel & Expense Claim

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F07 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years**

**Purpose:** to claim reimbursement, at actual cost with no markup, for travel pre-approved on Form F04.

**Governing references:** A2 §5.3 (travel), F04 (pre-approval). **Submit within 14 days of return.**

> **No pre-approval, no claim.** A claim that does not reference an approved F04 is not payable. That is not a technicality — it is the rule that keeps travel from becoming an unbudgeted line the customer did not agree to.

## Part 4 — Policy compliance

Confirm each:

☐ **Economy airfare** used for all flights
☐ **Standard business-class hotel** — no luxury or resort properties
☐ **Meals** claimed at the applicable US GSA per diem rate for the destination, or actual if lower
☐ **No alcohol** claimed
☐ **No entertainment** claimed
☐ **No personal expenses** claimed
☐ **No markup** applied to any item
☐ All receipts attached (required for every item)

> **The no-markup rule is the point of the whole arrangement.** We bill travel at cost because that is the only version a Class I procurement team accepts without question. A single marked-up airfare discovered in audit would call every other line into question, and the recoverable amount is not worth that.

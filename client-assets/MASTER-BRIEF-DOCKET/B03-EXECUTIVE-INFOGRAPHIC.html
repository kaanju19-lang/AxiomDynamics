Physics-derived condition assessment for freight rail

A train passes a detector. Something is computed. A person decides.

The system reads your wayside detector data, computes a physics-derived condition estimate for each asset, and ranks what it finds. It shows its arithmetic. Then it stops, and hands the assessment to a certified Railroad employee who decides what, if anything, to do. That handover is the design, not a limitation of it.

Not to us, not to a licence server, not to telemetry. The Railroad owns the box and the data on it.

What enters

Wayside detector payloads, in a schema you approve and we version. A bad reading produces no advisory and a log entry — not a weak advisory. A system that emits a faint signal for a broken sensor teaches reviewers to ignore faint signals.

What is computed

Given the same input, the same output, today and in five years. Nothing trains on your fleet. Every advisory carries the readings, the model outputs and the threshold basis that produced it, so a reviewer can recompute it rather than ratify it.

What is recorded

Exactly one disposition per advisory, with a reason and a reviewer. Coverage must be 100%. Four thousand advisories and three thousand dispositions is not improved review — it is nine hundred unexamined judgements, and the report will say so.

What this is not

  • Not a certified safety appliance. No FRA, Transport Canada or AAR approval.
  • Not a wayside defective bearing detector, and not a substitute for one.
  • Not a substitute for any inspection, test or maintenance action required by rule.
  • Not a train control, signal or protection system. It touches no interlocking.
  • Not an autonomous system. It issues no order and actuates nothing.
  • Not an alarm system of record. Regulatory alarms are your own detectors'.

Three controls are designed and not yet built. They are listed here, not in an appendix.

Append-only ledger
Today the write path can overwrite a record for the same advisory. The ledger is a record, not yet a tamper-evident trail. Rebuild target Q2 2027.
Registered identity
Reviewer identity is a local account, not a cryptographically issued credential. A hash proves integrity, not authorship. Target Q2 2027.
Signed export
Content hashes are produced; a signed bundle with partition boundary and record count is not. Target Q2 2027.

# F04 — Travel Pre-Approval Request

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F04 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years**

**Purpose:** to obtain written Customer approval, **before booking**, for any travel that will be billed to the Customer.

> **No pre-approval, no billable travel.** This is not a formality. It is the mechanism that keeps travel from becoming a surprise on an invoice, and it is the reason we can offer travel at cost with no markup without losing money on it.
>
> **Business development travel is never billable and never uses this form.** Flights to a corridor for a pre-contract conversation, a capability briefing, or a relationship meeting are our cost.

## Part 6 — After the trip

1. **Form F11** (Site Visit Report) is completed within 5 Business Days of return.
2. **Form F07** (Travel & Expense Claim) is submitted within 14 days, with receipts.
3. Any variance from the estimate is explained on F07.

**Unapproved variance:** if actual cost exceeds the approved cap, the excess is borne by AxiomDynamics and is not invoiced. This is stated here so that neither party is surprised.

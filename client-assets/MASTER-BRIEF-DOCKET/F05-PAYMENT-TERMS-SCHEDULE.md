# F05 — Payment Terms Schedule

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F05 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years**

**Purpose:** one page that states, unambiguously, what is paid, when, and how. Attached to every Order.

> **Why a separate form:** payment terms get agreed verbally and then disputed in month six. This form is the answer to "when is that invoice due?" and it is signed before any work starts.

**Governing reference:** A2 §10. **This form records a payment schedule for one Order; it does not vary A2.** Where this form and A2 §10 differ, **A2 governs** and this form is corrected. A form is an administrative instrument and cannot amend an agreement (F00 Part 1; D00 Part IV rule 8). The one thing this form decides that A2 leaves open is the **Order-specific instalment split and invoice dates**, which A2 §10 expressly delegates to the Order.

## Part 4 — Invoicing discipline

These four rules prevent the two most common payment failures.

**4.1 A valid invoice requires all of the following.** An invoice missing any item is not valid, and the 30-day clock does not start until it is complete.

1. Correct Customer legal entity name and billing address
2. Valid purchase order number, where required
3. Order number and milestone reference
4. Description matching the Order's fee line
5. Amount matching the Order

**4.2 Disputed invoices.** The Customer must notify a disputed line in writing within **15 days** of invoice date, stating the reason. Undisputed lines remain payable on time. A blanket withholding of the whole invoice for one disputed line is not permitted.

**4.3 No invoice without a PO.** Where the Customer requires a PO, invoicing without one is the single most common cause of a 60-day delay. **Obtain the PO at signature, not at invoicing.**

**4.4 Milestone triggers are objective.** An invoice is due when the trigger in Part 2 occurs. It is not due when someone gets round to confirming that it occurred. Where the trigger is a Customer signature (F02, F06), the Customer's delay in signing does not defer the obligation — **and where F06 provides for deemed acceptance, silence past 10 Business Days is acceptance.**

## Part 6 — What happens if payment is late

1. We notify the Customer's AP contact and the Order's business contact in writing.
2. If undisputed amounts remain unpaid **30 days after the due date**, we may suspend non-critical support after 10 Business Days' written notice.
3. **We do not suspend security patches, incident response, or data export for non-payment under any circumstances.** A customer with an unpatched platform is a risk to people, and that is not a commercial lever.
4. Continued non-payment is a material breach under D03 §12.2.

> **Point 3 is a deliberate commercial concession and should be stated to the customer.** It tells them what kind of vendor we are, and it costs nothing to promise because we would do it anyway.

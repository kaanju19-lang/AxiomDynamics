# F12 — Formal Notice

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F12 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years after the matter closes**

**Purpose:** the single form used for every formal notice under any agreement in this docket.

**Use for:** termination · material breach notice · cure notice · non-renewal election · conversion election (D05 §2.1) · dispute escalation (D07 §14) · insurance or assignment notice · any notice whose effectiveness depends on being in writing.

> **Why a separate form:** a contractual notice has a specific legal effect, and that effect usually depends on it being in writing, addressed correctly, and received on a date that can be proven. An email that says "we're terminating" to the wrong address, with no delivery proof, is often not a notice at all. This form makes the difference visible.
>
> **This form is not used for ordinary commercial correspondence.** Ticket updates, project emails, and routine questions do not need it.

## Part 4 — Delivery

**A notice is only a notice if it is delivered to the right address by a permitted method.**

### 4.1 Method of delivery

☐ **Hand delivery** — signature of recipient obtained (preferred: strongest proof)

☐ **Email with confirmed receipt** — to the address in §4.2, with acknowledgement obtained
☐ **Registered / certified post** — receipt number: __________

> **Do not use ordinary email and assume it worked.** For termination and breach notices, use hand delivery or courier with tracking, and follow with an email for speed. The email alone is the weakest method and is the one most often challenged.

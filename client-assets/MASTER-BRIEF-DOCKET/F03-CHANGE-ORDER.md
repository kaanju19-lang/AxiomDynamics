# F03 — Change Order

**AxiomDynamics LLC** · Form F03 · Version 1.0 · Effective ____________ · **Retain 7 years**

**Purpose:** the only mechanism by which scope, price, schedule, or acceptance criteria may change on an executed Order.

> **Nothing changes until this form is signed by both parties.** An email saying "sure, go ahead" is not a change order. Verbal agreement in a meeting is not a change order. This protects both sides equally: the Customer is not surprised by an invoice, and we are not performing unpaid work.

**Governing clause:** D03 §8. **Small-change tolerance:** work adding no more than 8 hours of effort, with no effect on price, schedule, or acceptance criteria, may proceed on written email confirmation from both authorised representatives. **Anything beyond that requires this form.**

> **What this Change Order may never do** (D04 Appendix D): vary the liability cap without an express clause reference; transfer IP ownership; create a performance guarantee; authorise operating advice; or amend D09 security obligations. If a requested change would do any of these, it is a renegotiation, not a change order, and must be handled separately.

## Part 4 — Work authorisation

**4.1** Work on this change may begin: ☐ On signature of this form ☐ On a stated date: __________

**4.2** Work must not begin before: ____________________

> **The supplier's protection clause.** No work on a change begins until this form is signed. If the Customer needs work to start sooner, they sign sooner. The frequency with which "we'll do the paperwork later" becomes a dispute is the reason this line exists.

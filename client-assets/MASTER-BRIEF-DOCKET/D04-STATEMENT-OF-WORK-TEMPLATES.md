# D04 — Statement of Work Templates

**Docket position:** D04. The executable orders. Each SOW is placed under D03 (MSA) and delivers a defined scope for a defined price.

**Classification:** Client-facing. Blank templates, completed per engagement.

> **Drafting status:** Commercial grade templates. Every SOW must be completed in full before signature — a blank field in an executed SOW is a dispute waiting to happen.

---

## How to use this document

There are five templates. Pick the one that matches what the customer is buying. Do not blend two into one — a mixed SOW is how scope disputes start.

| Template | Use when | Typical value |
|---|---|---|
| **SOW-1** | Pilot Implementation | $370,000 |
| **SOW-2** | Additional Site Deployment | $45,000/yr + licence |
| **SOW-3** | Feasibility & Physics Assessment (discovery) | $45,000–$75,000 |
| **SOW-4** | Prototype Development | $150,000–$400,000 |
| **SOW-5** | Professional Services / Training | Per A2 §5.5 |

**Three rules that apply to every SOW:**

1. **No SOW is valid without a signed D03 MSA in force.** If there is no MSA, the customer signs D03 first.
2. **Every fee must trace to A2.** If a number in an SOW is not in A2, A2 needs updating first, or the SOW states the deviation expressly.
3. **Every SOW has a defined end.** Either an end date, an acceptance event, or both. Open-ended SOWs are how projects become permanent.

# SOW-3 — Feasibility & Physics Assessment Statement of Work

**Order number:** SOW-3-________  **Placed under:** D03

**Customer:** ______________________  **Effective Date:** ____________

> **Use this template whenever a customer asks us to visit a corridor or a facility to look at a new failure mode.** Never travel on a verbal request. The observation visit is part of this Order, and this Order is signed before the flight is booked.

---

## 1. Background and question

**Failure mode or pain point under assessment:**

________________________________________________________________

**Asset class:** ________________  **Location(s):** ________________

**The question this assessment answers:**

> "Is this failure mode detectable from the data available, using physics-based methods, to a degree that would justify a production build — and if so, what would it take?"

**Both answers are valuable. "No, and here is why" is a legitimate and complete deliverable.**

## 2. Scope of work

| Phase | Activity | Location | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Kickoff observation visit — ride-along, interviews, data inventory, photographs | Customer site | 2–3 days |
| B | Data review — existing detector, sensor, maintenance, and failure records | Remote | 1–2 weeks |
| C | Physics analysis — model the failure mechanism; test feasibility against real data | Remote | 2–3 weeks |
| D | Report drafting and internal technical review | Remote | 1 week |
| E | Findings presentation and decision gate | Remote or on-site | 1 session |

**Total elapsed:** approximately **5–7 weeks** from F01-equivalent data receipt.

## 3. Deliverables

1. **Discovery Report** — the primary deliverable. Contents:
   - Restatement of the question and the data relied on
   - Physical mechanism analysis
   - Whether the signal is present in the available data, with evidence
   - Estimated detection envelope and its limitations
   - Data the Customer would need to supply for a production build
   - Recommended approach, with effort and risk
   - **A clear recommendation: proceed / proceed with conditions / do not proceed**
2. **Findings presentation** — one session, up to 90 minutes, for the Customer's technical stakeholders.
3. **Trip memoranda** — brief written notes from any observation visit.

## 4. Explicitly not included

1. Any prototype, model, or software build.
2. Any commitment to build, price, or schedule a prototype.
3. Any warranty that the failure mode is detectable.
4. Any right for the Customer to the underlying models, weights, or code (D13).
5. Any obligation on either party to proceed to SOW-4.

## 5. Fees

| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Feasibility & Physics Assessment — fixed fee | **$________** |
| Travel and expenses (estimated, pre-approved) | $________ |
| **Total not to exceed** | **$________** |

**Fee band:** $45,000–$75,000 per A2 §5.2, set by asset class, data availability, and site count.

**Payment:** 50% on signature, 50% on Discovery Report delivery.

## 6. Customer obligations

1. Provide access to the site(s) for the observation visit, including safety induction and any required PPE.
2. Provide the data listed in Appendix 1 within 10 Business Days of signature.
3. Designate a technical contact who can answer questions during the analysis.
4. Return Form F01 (site readiness) before the observation visit.

**If the data is not provided within 30 days**, the assessment timeline extends by the delay, and we may close the Order with the analysis possible on available data.

## 7. Intellectual property

1. **All IP arising from this assessment vests in AxiomDynamics**, including analysis methods, models, and the underlying physics approach (D13).
2. The Customer receives a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to **use the Discovery Report internally** for its own evaluation and planning.
3. The Customer's data remains the Customer's; we receive a limited licence to use it for this assessment only.
4. **We may reuse generalised knowledge** — what we learn about the physics of a failure mode, absent the Customer's confidential data or identifiers — with any future customer.
5. No exclusivity is granted. If the Customer wants exclusivity on a failure mode, that is a separate paid arrangement (A2 §5.5).

## 8. Confidentiality

Governed by D11. The observation visit requires a signed NDA **before** the visit occurs.

## 9. Decision gate

Within 30 days of report delivery the parties meet. The Customer may:
- **(a)** Proceed to SOW-4 prototype — we quote on the report's findings.
- **(b)** Hold — no obligation, no fee.
- **(c)** Close — no obligation, no fee.

**There is no penalty in any branch, and no obligation to proceed.** The Customer is buying information, not an option on our labour.

## Appendix — Rules common to all SOWs

### A. Completion of an Order

An Order completes on the earliest of: (a) all deliverables accepted; (b) the expiry date stated in the Order; (c) written agreement of both parties; (d) termination.

### B. Invoicing

Per the payment schedule in the Order and A2 §10. **No invoice without a valid purchase order number where the Customer requires one.**

### C. Records

Every Order is logged in the Forms Register (F00) with its number, date, value, and current state. Any Customer request that an Order exists but is not in the register should be treated as an error and investigated before work proceeds.

### D. What a SOW may never contain

A SOW may never: vary the liability cap without an express, specific clause reference; grant IP ownership to the Customer; create a performance guarantee; authorise operating advice; or amend D09 security obligations. If a Customer's procurement template demands any of these, that is a negotiation, not a drafting exercise.
